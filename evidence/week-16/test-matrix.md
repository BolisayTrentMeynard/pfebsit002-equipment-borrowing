## Deviations from the predictions (the misses stay in the record)

- **Baseline latency (chunk 3):** every p95 was above the predicted range, by 2.5 to 7 times. The fixed cost of about 600 ms per request was not predicted; OPcache turned out to be off.
- **Chunk 3 ordinary and spike:** median and php CPU lower than predicted; peak throughput slightly high; log growth lower; the spike recovery was faster than predicted (34 s, predicted 60 to 90 s).
- **Chunk 4:** detection time 99 s against 10 to 25 s predicted; 5xx latency (both hypotheses wrong); fewer user answers than predicted; ERROR count 22 against 30 to 100; recovery faster (6 s) than predicted.
- **Chunk 4b:** no misses on scored items (predictions written after chunk 4); the recovery was unclean.
- **Chunk 5:** C5-4 missed on the size of the drop (data endpoints fell more than `/up`); C5-5 missed on peak throughput (8.5 per s against 2.0 to 3.5) and on "far above 2,000 ms" (2,047.5 ms); C5-9 missed (`password` 2 matches, explained above); C5-2 had a bug in Claude's test.

## Failures, surprises and runs that do not count

- Not counted: baseline attempt 1 (sizing); chunk 4 attempts 1 and 2 (no password variable; control run with no outage); chunk 4b first attempt (accidental Start); chunk 5 two ordinary starts (wrong folder, `LOAD_TEST_PASSWORD` not set); four rollback Starts (login account missing because the reset had removed the load-test accounts, a window without the password, Caddy not running, a leftover rollback folder).
- Unexplained and left unexplained: Apache stopped and started with MySQL in the chunk 4 first run; MySQL stop and start at 21:11 in chunk 4b; MySQL's two crashes after the 4b Start; why 6 more writes appear in the spike database than k6 counted (likely requests still queued when k6 stopped).
- The chunk 5 sampler files `ordinary-after-resources.csv` and `peak-after-resources.csv` were not read, so the php CPU after the change is not reported.
